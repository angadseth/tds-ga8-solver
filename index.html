Q1–Q7 are servers, not values

Seven of the ten questions grade a URL: the marker posts a fixed set of requests to an endpoint and scores how it answers. So this site runs one, and gives you its address on your own path — the same URL for all seven boxes. Enter your email above and it appears with the rest of your answers.

The path matters more than it looks. Q2 remembers each select's run so the evaluate that follows can check the lineage against it, and Q5 remembers each freeze so a replay with different input can refuse it. Put every student on one bare URL and those memories collide the moment two people grade at once. Keyed by the email in the path, they do not.

Prefer to run your own? server/ is the same code, one click to deploy, and the solver panel takes your URL instead.

Q8 · LoRA budget
Rebuilds the PEFT adapter's safetensors layout byte for byte, so the file size is counted, not estimated.
Q9 · Training fingerprint
Replays the whole training loop in float64 — SGD, AdamW or RMSprop, whichever your seed picked, on a cosine or step schedule.
Q10 · Carbon audit
Energy and CO₂ from the exam's own TDP and grid-intensity tables, not from public datasheets.
